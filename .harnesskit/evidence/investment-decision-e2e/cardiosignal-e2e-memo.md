## Company and financing

CardioSignal is seeking an $8 million Series A to develop an AI-enabled cardiovascular diagnostics platform for detecting atrial fibrillation and deterioration associated with heart failure. The company reports $240,000 of annualized recurring revenue from two paid hospital pilots.

## Clinical evidence claimed by management

Management reports a prospective 420-patient study conducted across three hospitals. The pitch states 91% sensitivity and 87% specificity against a prespecified primary endpoint. No hospital names, principal investigators, protocol registration, independent statistical analysis, subgroup results, confidence intervals, adverse-event analysis, manuscript, or raw validation data were supplied.

The pitch describes use of wearable ECG and longitudinal clinical data, but does not identify supported devices, sampling requirements, missing-data handling, adjudication procedures, or whether the evaluation used a locked model on a fully held-out population.
